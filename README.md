# 🎼 Fauna & Orchestra

[![Minecraft Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/Minecraft-1.21.1-green.svg)](https://minecraft.net)
[![Mod Loader](https://img.shields.io/badge/NeoForge-1.21.1-orange.svg)](https://neoforged.net/)
[![Dependencies](https://img.shields.io/badge/Dependencies-Geckolib%20%7C%20Patchouli-blueviolet.svg)](https://github.com/migueel26/Fauna-And-Orchestra)

**Fauna & Orchestra** is a magical exploration mod that harmonizes the natural world with the power of music. Embark on a journey to assemble a symphonic menagerie, master musical sorcery, and protect the world from the looming threat of the **Great Composer**.

---

## 🎻 Features

### 🐾 Collect Your Musicians
Discover unique animals across the world and equip them with specialized instruments. Each creature brings its own "voice" to your orchestra, providing different buffs, abilities, and musical interactions.

### 🪄 Musical Magic & Research
Dive deep into the arcane arts of sound. Use the **Patchouli-powered Guidebook** to research musical spells, craft harmonic blocks, and unlock powerful abilities that resonate with the environment.

### 🎭 The Great Composer Saga
The world is in disharmony. Face off against new NPCs and prepare for the ultimate crescendo: a battle against the **Great Composer**, a powerful entity threatening to overwrite the world's natural melody.

### 🎹 Content Overview
* **Custom Entities:** From the tiny *Sproutlings* to the elusive *Wandering Koalas*.
* **Dynamic Music System:** Instruments that actually play and interact with the world.
* **Unique Blocks & Items:** Decorative and functional blocks inspired by classical and folk instruments.
* **GeckoLib Animations:** Fully animated 3D models for a more immersive experience.

## 🛠 Installation

### Requirements
* **Minecraft:** 1.21.1
* **Mod Loader:** [NeoForge](https://neoforged.net/)
* **Required Dependencies:**
    * [GeckoLib](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/geckolib)
    * [Patchouli](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/patchouli)

### Steps
1. Download the latest `.jar` from the [Releases](https://github.com/migueel26/Fauna-And-Orchestra/releases) page.
2. Ensure you have NeoForge installed for version 1.21.1.
3. Drop the mod file and its dependencies into your `.minecraft/mods` folder.
4. Launch the game and start your overture!

---

## 📖 Getting Started
Once in-game, your first goal is to craft the **Orchestral Codex** (Patchouli Guide). This book will lead you through the first steps of taming musical fauna and crafting your first set of instruments.

---

## 🏗 Development
This mod is currently in active development on the `develop` branch.

### Building from Source
If you wish to contribute or test the latest features:
1. Clone the repository: `git clone -b develop https://github.com/migueel26/Fauna-And-Orchestra.git`
2. Run `./gradlew genIntellijRuns` (o tu IDE equivalente).
3. Build the mod using `./gradlew build`.
